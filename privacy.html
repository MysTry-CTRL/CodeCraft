Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

CodeCraft Academy helps young people learn programming through guided projects. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to students, parents, and guardians.

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and password. We also collect information you choose to provide while using the lessons, such as project progress or messages sent to our support team.

We do not ask students to share sensitive personal information. Please do not post your home address, phone number, school name, photographs, or other private details in a project or message.

2. How we use information

We use account information to create and secure your account, provide lessons, remember your progress, respond to questions, and improve CodeCraft. We do not sell personal information or use it to show advertising.

3. Children and guardians

CodeCraft is designed for learners ages 11 to 14. A parent or legal guardian should review this policy and help a child create an account. If you believe a child has shared personal information without permission, please contact us so we can review and remove it.

4. Sharing and security

We only share information when needed to provide the service, follow the law, or protect the safety of our users. We take reasonable steps to protect account information, but no online service can guarantee complete security. Choose a unique password and never share it with anyone.

5. Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your account information. You can also stop using CodeCraft at any time. To request help with your information, contact the CodeCraft team through the account support channel.

6. Policy updates

We may update this policy when CodeCraft changes. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after an update means that you have read the revised policy.

Questions about privacy? Return to CodeCraft or speak with a parent or guardian before creating an account.
